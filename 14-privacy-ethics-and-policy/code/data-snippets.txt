DATA REDLINE — WORKPLACE SNIPPETS TO CLASSIFY
The AI-Powered Professional · Chapter 14 · Privacy, Ethics, and Organizational Policy

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO USE THIS FILE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Below are 12 short, realistic things that cross a working professional's desk in
a normal week. Imagine you are about to paste one of them into a PUBLIC consumer
AI chatbot (a free or personal-tier ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini account) to get
help — rewrite it, summarize it, translate it, draft a reply.

For EACH snippet, decide one of three verdicts and write a one-line reason:

  SAFE    — no confidential, personal, or regulated data; fine to paste as-is
            into a public tool.
  REDACT  — useful to paste ONLY after you remove or replace the sensitive
            parts (names, IDs, numbers, the client's identity) with
            placeholders like [CLIENT], [NAME], [ACCOUNT #]. The *shape* of the
            task is fine; the *specifics* are not.
  NEVER   — do not paste this into a public tool in any form. It is regulated,
            secret, or identifying enough that redaction cannot save it. Use an
            approved enterprise tool, a private/local model, or do it by hand.

There is no answer key in this file on purpose — the judgment IS the rep.
Bring your verdicts to the Graded Lab on the Chapter 14 exercises page.
