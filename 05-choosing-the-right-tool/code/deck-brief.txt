TITLE
  Riverbend Regional Food Bank — Q2 Board Update

THE HEADLINE NUMBERS (Q2 vs Q1)
  - Households served:            8,410   (up from 7,220 in Q1, +16.5%)
  - Meals distributed:            312,000 (up from 270,000, +15.6%)
  - Cost per household served:    $34.10  (down from $37.80, -9.8%)
  - Volunteer hours:              6,140   (down from 6,900, -11%)
  - Total spend:                  $286,800 (up from $272,900, +5.1%)

WHAT DROVE IT
  - New Tuesday mobile-pantry stop at Eastside added ~900 households.
  - A produce-rescue partnership cut food cost per meal.
  - We did MORE with SLIGHTLY more money — efficiency, not just spending.

THE HONEST SOFT SPOT (do not hide this)
  - Volunteer hours fell 11%. Two long-time coordinators moved away.
    We covered it with paid part-time help, which is part of the +5% spend.
    This is not yet a crisis, but it is the trend to watch.

THE ASK (the reason for the meeting)
  - Approve $18,500 for a second walk-in cooler.
  - Why: produce-rescue volume already exceeds current cold storage; we
    turned away ~2 pallets of donated produce in June for lack of space.
  - Payback: keeps cost-per-meal falling; protects the produce partnership.

NEXT QUARTER (Q3) PLAN, ONE LINE EACH
  - Launch a Thursday Eastside stop (mirror the Tuesday win).
  - Recruit 15 volunteers to reverse the hours decline.
  - Install the cooler by end of Q3 if approved tonight.

CLOSE
  - Thank the board. Restate the one thing: more people, steady cost, one
    cooler to keep it going.
