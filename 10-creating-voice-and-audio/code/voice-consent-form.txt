SYNTHETIC VOICE — CONSENT & DISCLOSURE FORM  (starter template — adapt it, make it yours)
============================================================================================

Purpose: No voice should be cloned, generated, or published without the informed, written
consent of the person whose voice it is. This form is the record that consent was given, for
what, and within what limits. In this course you may only clone YOUR OWN voice — so you are
both the "Voice Owner" and the "Requester." Fill it out anyway: the habit is the point.

2. REQUESTER / PROJECT
   Your name: ______________________________________
   Project or purpose: _____________________________________________________________
   Tool used (e.g., ElevenLabs): ___________________________________________________

3. SCOPE OF CONSENT  (check every use you are authorizing — leave the rest UNCHECKED)
   [ ] A single, specific recording of the provided script, for this class only
   [ ] Internal, non-public use only
   [ ] Public publication (specify where): __________________________________________
   [ ] Ongoing / reusable voice model
   Duration of consent (e.g., "this assignment only", "12 months"): _________________

4. HARD LIMITS  (these are NEVER authorized, regardless of the above)
   - No impersonation intended to deceive any listener into thinking it is a live person.
   - No financial, legal, medical, or emergency instructions in the cloned voice.
   - No use that could harm, defame, or embarrass the Voice Owner.
   - No transfer of the voice model to a third party without a new signed consent.

5. DISCLOSURE  (how listeners will be told the audio is synthetic)
   Method (spoken tag / on-screen label / caption / description): ___________________
   Exact disclosure wording: _______________________________________________________
   ("This audio uses an AI-generated voice." is a fine default.)

6. REVOCATION

   and any generated audio must be deleted. Revocation contact: ____________________

Reminder from Appendix C: consent + disclosure are the two non-negotiables. If either box
is empty, you are not clear to proceed. You choose the tool; you own the verdict — and here,
you also owe a duty to the person whose voice it is.
