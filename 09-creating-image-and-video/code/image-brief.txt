=============================================================
CREATIVE BRIEF — "Spring Open House" image set
Client: Fair Oaks Garden Center (family-owned plant nursery)
Prepared for: whoever is making the images (that's you)
Version: 1.0
=============================================================

THE JOB
-------
We are holding our Spring Open House on the first Saturday in May.
We need a small, on-brand set of images for a printed flyer, our
social posts, and the header of one email. We have a tiny budget and
no photographer this year. We want to try an AI image tool.

Your job is to GENERATE these images on brief, iterate the prompts
until they fit, and hand back a set we could actually publish -- with
an honest note about the rights and how we should label them.


WHAT WE SELL / WHO WE ARE
-------------------------
- A friendly, third-generation neighborhood garden center.
- Not a big-box store. Warm, local, a little rustic, hands-in-the-dirt.
- Customers are home gardeners: families, retirees, first-time
  balcony gardeners. Ages roughly 30-75.
- Brand feeling in three words: WARM, GROWING, TRUSTWORTHY.


DELIVERABLES (this is the set you must produce)
-----------------------------------------------
1. HERO IMAGE  -- for the top of the printed flyer.
   Aspect ratio: 4:5 (portrait). Subject: an inviting nursery
   scene -- rows of spring seedlings, hanging baskets, morning light.
   No readable text baked into the image (we add the words ourselves).

2. SOCIAL SQUARE -- for Instagram/Facebook.
   Aspect ratio: 1:1 (square). Subject: a close, joyful moment --
   a pair of hands potting a young tomato plant, soil visible.

3. EMAIL BANNER -- wide header for one announcement email.
   Aspect ratio: 16:9 (landscape). Subject: a welcoming storefront /
   entrance with flowers, a chalkboard-style sign area left blank.

You may generate extra variations, but these THREE are the required set.


MOOD & LOOK (the style levers)
------------------------------
- STYLE:       natural, photographic, editorial -- NOT cartoon,
               NOT glossy stock-photo, NOT 3D render.
- LIGHTING:    soft, warm morning light; gentle shadows; no harsh flash.
- COLOR:       greens, terracotta/clay, warm wood, cream. Avoid neon.
- COMPOSITION: uncluttered; room to add our own text; a clear subject.
- FEELING:     approachable and real, like a good local newsletter photo.


DO / DON'T
----------
DO:
  - Keep it believable and honest -- this should look like OUR nursery
    could look, not a fantasy garden.
  - Leave clean, uncluttered space where we will add words later.
  - Give us more than one option per deliverable if you can.

DON'T:
  - No recognizable real people's faces (no depicting a real named
    person, staff member, or celebrity likeness).
  - No brand logos, no competitor signage, no trademarked characters.
  - No text baked into the image that we can't edit (garbled AI text
    looks worse than no text).
  - Nothing we'd be embarrassed to have a customer find out was AI.


WHAT TO HAND BACK
-----------------
- The final image for each of the three deliverables (exported files
  or clean screenshots).
- The prompt you used for each, and the earlier prompt versions you
  iterated through (v1 -> v2 -> v3) with a one-line note on what you
  changed and why.
- The completed image-rights-checklist.txt for the set.
- A short, honest note: which tool you used, whether we may use these
  commercially, and how you recommend we label them.

Thank you. Make it feel like us.
-- The Fair Oaks family
