WHY THIS SHEET EXISTS
  You do not memorize a landscape that changes weekly. You learn the
  SHAPE of it: a handful of labs, each shipping a FAMILY of models on a
  ladder from cheap-and-fast to expensive-and-powerful. Learn the ladder,
  not the leaf. When a name here is stale, the pattern will still be true.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE FRONTIER LABS (who makes the "engines" behind the apps)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  OpenAI ........ GPT-5.x family. Powers ChatGPT. The default all-rounder;
                  largest reach. Strong writing, images (GPT Image 2),
                  voice, agentic "computer use."
  Anthropic ..... Claude family. Careful reasoning, long documents, top
                  coding. Reputation for trust/precision. ~1M-token
                  context on the flagship.
  Google ........ Gemini 3.x family. Best native MULTIMODAL (reads video +
                  audio directly) and deep Google Workspace integration
                  (Gmail/Docs/Sheets). ~1M-token context.
  xAI ........... Grok 4.6 (+ Grok Imagine for image/video). Real-time
                  info via live web/X; cheaper tokens; looser content
                  rules.
  Meta .......... Llama 4 (Scout / Maverick). OPEN-WEIGHT — free to
                  download and self-host. (Llama 4 "Behemoth" was
                  previewed but never shipped — do not treat as available.)
  Mistral ....... Mistral Large 3. European; open-weight-friendly
                  (Apache-2.0 license on several models).
  DeepSeek ...... DeepSeek V4. Open-weight (MIT); near-frontier quality at
                  a fraction of the cost. Chinese lab — raise
                  data-residency questions for sensitive work.
  Alibaba ....... Qwen3 family. Strong OPEN-WEIGHT models + embeddings.
  Microsoft ..... Phi (small, efficient, on-device-friendly) + MAI
                  in-house models. Also resells the others via Azure.

THE FAMILY LADDER (every lab ships a version of this)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  NANO / TINY .... cheapest, fastest, high-volume, simple work.
  MINI / SMALL ... cost-efficient; good for most everyday tasks.
  BALANCED ....... the everyday workhorse; the sane default.
  FLAGSHIP ....... hard, multi-step work; long documents; agents.
  REASONING / PRO  "thinks" step-by-step before answering; deep research,
                   multi-step problems, careful analysis. Slower, pricier.
  REALTIME/VOICE   tuned for live spoken conversation and low latency.
  IMAGE / VIDEO .. separate generation models (Chapter 9 covers these).

THE FIVE TRADEOFF DIALS (turn these before you choose)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. DIFFICULTY .. hard reasoning/long docs -> flagship or reasoning tier.
                   simple/routine -> mini or nano is usually plenty.
  2. SPEED ....... need it now / live chat -> smaller, faster tier.
  3. COST ........ high volume or tight budget -> smaller tier. Flagships
                   cost 10-100x a nano per token; output dominates the bill.
  4. PRIVACY ..... sensitive/confidential -> a governed enterprise tool or
                   a LOCAL model (Chapter 11). Never paste PII/PHI into a
                   consumer chatbot. (See Appendix C.)
  5. MULTIMODAL .. images/audio/video in or out -> pick a model built for
                   it (Gemini for native video+audio; image tools in Ch 9).


------------------------------------------------------------------------
OPEN vs CLOSED (preview — hands-on local is Chapter 11)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  CLOSED / COMMERCIAL ... GPT, Claude, Gemini. You rent them (API or a
                          subscription); the weights stay with the lab.
                          Newest, largest, usually smartest.
  OPEN-WEIGHT ........... Llama, Mistral, DeepSeek, Qwen, Phi. You can
                          download and run them yourself -> privacy,
                          offline, no per-token fee. Tradeoff: usually
                          less capable than the top closed flagships, and
                          you need decent hardware.
  NOTE .................. "open-weight" is NOT the same as "open source" —
                          you get the trained model, rarely the training
                          data. Licenses differ (Apache-2.0, MIT, and
                          model-specific community licenses). Read them.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONSUMER PLANS (how most professionals actually pay — mid-2026)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Free tiers ......... real and capable, but rate-limited and often on a
                       smaller model. Fine for learning.
  ~$20/month ......... ChatGPT Plus, Claude Pro, Google AI Pro (~$19.99,
                       bundles NotebookLM Plus), Perplexity Pro, Grok.
  ~$100-250/month .... "Pro / Max / Ultra" heavy-use tiers (verify).
  These numbers move. SEND YOURSELF to the vendor's pricing page.
