  HOW TO USE THIS FILE
  1. Copy it into your own doc (Google Docs / Word / a plain note).
  2. Fill PART 1 by hand, BEFORE you ask any AI to help. Look at your
     real week, not the week you wish you had.
  3. Then work through PART 2 (triage), PART 3 (automation), PART 4
     (one designed workflow), and PART 5 (measure).
  4. Keep it. This template is the backbone of the graded lab.

  KEY: shape  = RULE-shaped (describable procedure, checkable output)
                or JUDGMENT-shaped (it depends, and you decide)
       bucket = SELF  (do it yourself, AI stays out)
                ASSIST (delegate to AI, then verify)
                NEVER  (never delegate / never paste — policy, law,
                        confidential data, or a call only you may make)
