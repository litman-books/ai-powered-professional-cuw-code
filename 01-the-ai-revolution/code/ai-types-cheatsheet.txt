HOW TO READ THIS SHEET
----------------------
These six "types" are not airtight scientific categories. They are
practical LENSES. One real product is usually several at once: the
assistant on your phone can be conversational AND multimodal AND
agentic in the same minute. The point of naming the type is not to
win an argument — it is to know WHAT THE THING IS DOING so you know
WHAT TO VERIFY. Different types fail in different ways.

The cleanest real line is between PREDICTIVE (it scores or sorts
something that already exists) and GENERATIVE (it makes something
new). The other four are mostly flavors of generative systems.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
1) PREDICTIVE AI  —  "It guesses a label or a number."
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Does:      Learns patterns from past data, then forecasts, scores,
           ranks, or classifies new cases. Output is a judgment about
           existing data, not new content.
Everyday:  Your bank texts "Was this you?" on a strange charge.
           The spam folder. The "you might also like" row.
Products:  Fraud detection, credit scoring, weather models,
           recommendation feeds, predictive maintenance.
Watch for: Bias baked into old data; a confident score on a case
           it never really saw before.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
2) GENERATIVE AI  —  "It makes new content."
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Does:      Produces original-looking text, images, audio, video, or
           code from a prompt.
Everyday:  You type "write a thank-you note to a donor" and get a
           finished draft. You type "a golden retriever as an
           astronaut" and get a picture.
Products:  ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini (text); image and video tools.
Watch for: HALLUCINATION — fluent, confident, and simply wrong. The
           tone never tells you which sentences are true.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
3) CONVERSATIONAL AI  —  "It talks back, in turns."
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Does:      Holds a natural back-and-forth: remembers the last few
           messages, answers follow-ups, asks clarifying questions.
Everyday:  The chat window where you can say "make it shorter" and
           "now more formal" without re-explaining everything.
Products:  The chat mode of ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini; voice
           assistants; customer-service chatbots.
Watch for: It sounds like a person, so you trust it like a person.
           It is a very good pattern-matcher, not a colleague.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
4) MULTIMODAL AI  —  "It reads and makes more than text."
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Does:      Takes in and/or produces several kinds of media — text,
           images, audio, video — in one system.
Everyday:  Snap a photo of a rash, a menu, or a broken part and ask
           "what am I looking at / what do I do?" Point it at a
           chart and ask what it says.
Products:  Assistants that accept photos, screenshots, PDFs, voice.
Watch for: It can misread an image as confidently as it misreads a
           question. Verify what it claims to "see."

--------------------------------------------------------------------
5) AGENTIC AI  —  "It takes multi-step action toward a goal."
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Does:      Plans a sequence of steps, uses tools (browse, click,
           fill forms, send), and works toward an outcome with less
           step-by-step hand-holding.
Everyday:  "Find three dog-friendly hotels near the venue under $180
           and put them in a table" — and it goes and does it.
Products:  "Agent" / "computer use" modes; research agents that
           browse many sources and write a cited report.
Watch for: It can take REAL ACTIONS (spend, send, book). Delegation
           never transfers accountability. Keep a human approval
           gate on anything that costs money or leaves the building.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
6) PHYSICAL / ROBOTIC AI  —  "It has a body in the world."
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Does:      AI embodied in hardware that senses and acts on the
           physical world.
Everyday:  The robot vacuum. The self-driving feature. The warehouse
           picker. Early humanoid robots.
Products:  Autonomous vehicles, factory/warehouse robots, drones,
           surgical-assist systems.
Watch for: Failures here can hurt people or property, not just an
           inbox. Highest stakes, strictest oversight.

====================================================================
THE ONE RULE THAT SURVIVES EVERY VERSION NUMBER
--------------------------------------------------------------------
You choose the tool. You own the verdict.
AI drafts, generates, and accelerates. The professional decides,
verifies, and is accountable. AI is an assistant, not an authority.
