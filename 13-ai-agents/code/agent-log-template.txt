AGENT LOG  —  Project 13: Delegate to an Agent
The AI-Powered Professional
========================================================================

An agent-log is the record of a delegation. It is the thing you can hand
to your boss, your client, or an auditor and say: "Here is exactly what I
asked the machine to do, what it did, where it was wrong, where I stepped
in, and why the final result is mine." It is the paper trail of your
accountability. Fill in EVERY section. "N/A" is an answer; a blank is not.

Keep this file in plain language. You do not need to be technical. You
need to be honest and specific.
